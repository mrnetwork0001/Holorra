TITLE
Holorra: the 3D world is generated. Now it is alive. (Manycore Aholo Global 3D AI Hackathon)

DESCRIPTION
AI can build a 3D world in minutes. Then you step inside, and nothing moves. Holorra is one browser tab where Manycore's Aholo tools become a single scene you can play: generate the world, split the parts, rig the actors, direct the scene.

Play it: https://holorra.xyz
Studio: https://holorra.xyz/studio?play=1
Real Lux3D results, inspected live: https://holorra.xyz/lab
Project report: https://holorra.xyz/report

Everything on screen is the live site. The Text to 3D job, the Play run and the phone session are our own screen recordings, with the waiting cut.

Built on @manycore/aholo-viewer, aholo-sdk-world, aholo-sdk-lux3d, aholo-sdk-asset and aholo-sdk-mcp.
